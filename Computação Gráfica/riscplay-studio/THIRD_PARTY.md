Riscplay Studio reuses the console/audio headers, game sources and packaged assets supplied by Mario Gazziro; these are not assigned a new license. asset_manager.py derives from the supplied 8bitworkshop-riscplay/riscplay-local/asset_manager.py, with SDRAM capacity adjusted to the stable hardware. The 8bitworkshop license is included as LICENSE_8BITWORKSHOP.

The editor uses CodeMirror 6 and its C language package, Lezer, crelt, style-mod and w3c-keyname from the supplied 8bitworkshop archive. Their individual license files are retained under dist/vendor. No remote fonts or CDN scripts are required.
